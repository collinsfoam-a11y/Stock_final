## 2023-10-26 - Accessible Search Autocomplete
**Learning:** The React Native `TextInput` used for search and custom autocomplete list items (often implemented with custom Touchables) require specific accessibility roles (e.g., `accessibilityRole="search"` and `accessibilityRole="button"`) and hints to be properly navigated by screen readers.
**Action:** Always ensure search inputs and their corresponding custom dropdown items are explicitly labeled and typed for accessibility.
